I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Chemical & Biomolecular Engineering at the University of California, Berkeley. My research encompasses the use of modern computational biology pipelines for protein design in bacterial immune systems. 

Previously, I graduated from the University of California, Davis with a degree in Biochemical Engineering. My undergraduate research work with [the McDonald-Nandi Group](https://mcdonald-nandi.ech.ucdavis.edu/) focused on bioprocess engineering and the characterization of recombinant protein therapeutics in plant expression systems.
